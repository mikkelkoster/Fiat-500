## Description (4000)
Klar makes the car's remote features quick and calm to use.

CLIMATE
One tap warms or cools the car to the temperature last set inside it, and a live countdown shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The temperature outside the car is right there to help you decide. Stop it any time, even while the car is still answering.

SCHEDULES
Set "ready by 07:30 on weekdays" and Klar starts the climate in time. Make as many as you need, for weekdays and weekends.

BATTERY AND RANGE
A clear gauge shows charge and range, coloured by how much is left. Swipe between them; pull down to refresh.

LOCK, UNLOCK, LIGHTS, BOOT, FIND
Slide to lock or unlock. Flash the lights to spot the car in a car park, unlock the boot, and see where the car is on a map with walking directions.

ALERTS
Optional notifications when charging finishes, the battery runs low or the car has been left unlocked.

EVERYWHERE YOU ARE
Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center buttons, Siri and Shortcuts, and an Apple Watch app with complications.

YOUR ACCOUNT STAYS YOURS
Klar signs in with the account you already use in your car brand's app. Your login stays in your iPhone's keychain and is sent only to your car brand's servers. Klar has no servers of its own and collects nothing.

WHAT YOU NEED
A connected car and its brand's remote-services subscription (for example My Remote). Reading the battery is usually free; climate, locking and finding the car need the subscription. Try everything first with the sample car, no account needed.

Klar is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by any car maker. Brand names belong to their owners.
