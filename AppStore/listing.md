## Description (4000)
Varme makes the car's remote features quick and calm to use.

HEAT THE CABIN
One tap starts warming the car, and a live countdown shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Stop it any time, even while the car is still answering.

SCHEDULES
Set "warm by 07:30 on weekdays" and Varme starts the heat in time. Make as many as you need, for weekdays and weekends, and switch the car's own charging and climate timers on or off.

BATTERY AND RANGE
A clear gauge shows charge and range, coloured by how much is left. Swipe between them; pull down to refresh.

LOCK, UNLOCK, LIGHTS, BOOT, FIND
Slide to lock or unlock. Flash the lights to spot the car in a car park, unlock the boot, and see where the car is on a map with walking directions.

ALERTS
Optional notifications when charging finishes, the battery runs low or the car has been left unlocked.

EVERYWHERE YOU ARE
Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center buttons, Siri and Shortcuts, and an Apple Watch app with complications.

YOUR ACCOUNT STAYS YOURS
Varme signs in with the account you already use in your car brand's app. Your login stays in your iPhone's keychain and is sent only to your car brand's servers. Varme has no servers of its own and collects nothing.

WHAT YOU NEED
A connected car and its brand's remote-services subscription (for example My Remote). Reading the battery is usually free; heating, locking and finding the car need the subscription. Try everything first with the sample car, no account needed.
