Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026 (weather added)

Short version: Luft collects nothing. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your car account

To control your car, Luft signs in with the account you already use in your car brand's app. Your email, password and PIN are stored in your iPhone's keychain and are sent only to your car brand's own servers, to sign in and to carry out what you ask (for example, start heating). They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Your car's information

Battery, range, lock state, position, trips and the picture of your car are read from your car brand's servers and kept on your iPhone (and, if you use them, your Apple Watch and widgets). They are not sent anywhere else.

Your location

If you allow it, Luft uses your iPhone's location on the device to show how far away your car is and to open walking directions in Apple Maps. Your location is not stored or sent anywhere by Luft.

Weather

To show the temperature outside your car, Luft asks Apple Weather for the weather at the car's last known position. Apple receives that position for the lookup under its own privacy policy; it isn't sent to the developer or anyone else. Cars that report their own temperature don't need the lookup.

Notifications and schedules

Schedules, alerts and settings are stored on your iPhone. Notifications are created on the device.

Your car brand

Your car brand processes your account and car data under its own privacy policy, as it does when you use its app.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Settings to remove your login from the keychain, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

Luft is not directed at children.
