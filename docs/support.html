What do I need?

A connected car and the account you use in your car brand's app. The climate, locking and finding the car need the brand's remote-services subscription (for example My Remote); reading the battery is usually free.

Sign-in doesn't work

Use the same email, password and PIN as in your car brand's app. Klar finds the region your car was sold in by itself. If the brand's own app can't reach the car, Klar can't either.

A button is greyed out

Your car or subscription doesn't offer that feature. Once you add the subscription, pull down on the home screen to refresh.

The numbers are old

Klar shows what your car last reported to its brand's servers, and reads them again every minute while the app is open. Pull down to refresh at any time. If the car hasn't reported for a while, Klar asks it to wake up and send fresh numbers, which can take a minute. Some cars don't allow that; their numbers update when the car next reports by itself, for example after a drive or when charging starts or stops.

Warming or cooling?

Climate runs the car's own climate at the temperature last set inside the car: it warms the cabin when it's cold and cools it when it's hot. Set the temperature in the car before you leave it, and Klar uses that.

Schedules

iOS decides exactly when apps may run in the background, so Klar also sends a notification at the start time; tap Start now on it if the climate hasn't started. For a fully hands-off schedule, use a Shortcuts automation with the Start Car Climate action.

Try it without a car

On the first screen, tap Try with a sample car.
