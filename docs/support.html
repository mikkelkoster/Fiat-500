What do I need?

A connected car and the account you use in your car brand's app. Heating, locking and finding the car need the brand's remote-services subscription (for example My Remote); reading the battery is usually free.

Sign-in doesn't work

Use the same email, password and PIN as in your car brand's app, and pick the region your car was sold in. If the brand's own app can't reach the car, Varme can't either.

A button is greyed out

Your car or subscription doesn't offer that feature. Once you add the subscription, pull down on the home screen to refresh.

The numbers are old

Pull down to refresh. If the car hasn't reported for a while, Varme also asks the car to wake up and send fresh numbers, which can take a minute.

Schedules

iOS decides exactly when apps may run in the background, so Varme also sends a notification at the start time; tap Heat now on it if heating hasn't started. For a fully hands-off schedule, use a Shortcuts automation with the Heat Car action.

Try it without a car

On the first screen, tap Try with a sample car.
